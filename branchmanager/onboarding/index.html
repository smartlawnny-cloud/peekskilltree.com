I-9 Employment Eligibility FEDERAL
2 min · Bring valid ID
›

Federal law requires every employee to prove they're authorized to work in the U.S. This is the actual federal form — cannot be replaced with a checkbox.

  • What to bring: Driver's license + Social Security card, OR a valid U.S. passport
  • Deadline: Must be completed within 3 business days of your first day
  • Employer retains the signed I-9 for 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination (whichever is later)

Fall Protection OSHA
15 min video · #1 cause of tree worker deaths
›
  • Fall protection required at 6 feet (general industry)
  • Climbing saddle + climbing line = your fall protection in the tree
  • Bucket truck: full body harness + lanyard to anchor point
  • Inspect all gear daily — no exceptions
Falls are the #1 killer of tree workers. 45% of all tree care fatalities.

🎓 Optional Certifications (Extra Credit)

These aren't required but they save money on insurance, win more bids, and increase your pay rate. Doug covers the cost for active employees.

🌳 ISA Certified Arborist
International Society of Arboriculture. Gold standard for tree care. Study online, take proctored exam.
Benefits: Higher pay rate · Required for arborist letters ($250 each) · Insurance discount · Marketing ("ISA Certified" on trucks/website) · Win municipal contracts
Cost: ~$200 exam · Study: 2-4 months · Renew: every 3 years (30 CEUs)
ISA Website →
🪢 TCIA Certified Treecare Safety Professional (CTSP)
Tree Care Industry Association safety certification. Online course + exam.
Benefits: Up to 10% workers' comp insurance discount · OSHA compliance documentation · Marketing credibility
Cost: ~$500 · Study: 2-3 days online · Renew: every 3 years
TCIA CTSP →
🏗️ OSHA 10-Hour Construction
Free online through OSHA Education Center. Covers fall protection, electrical, excavation, PPE. 10 hours total.
Benefits: OSHA card (wallet) · Required for some municipal jobs · Insurance documentation · Shows due diligence
Cost: Free – $25 for card · Time: 10 hours online at your own pace
OSHA Outreach →
🔗 EHAP — Electrical Hazards Awareness Program
TCIA program specifically for tree workers near power lines. Online course.
Benefits: Required by many utilities for line-clearance work · Insurance documentation · Can bid on utility contracts
Cost: ~$35 · Time: 2-3 hours online
TCIA EHAP →
🚑 First Aid / CPR / AED
American Red Cross or American Heart Association. Online + skills check.
Benefits: OSHA recommends for all field workers · Insurance documentation · Can save a life
Cost: ~$35 online · Time: 4-5 hours · Renew: every 2 years
Red Cross →
