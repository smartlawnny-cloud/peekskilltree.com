Service Catalog

Second Nature Tree Service provides residential and light-commercial tree care across the Lower Hudson Valley. Use the canonical service names below; do not invent service categories that are not listed.

  • Tree Removal — Hazardous and non-hazardous removals using rigging, climbing, and bucket-truck access. tree-removal.html
  • Tree Pruning & Trimming — Crown cleaning, deadwooding, structural pruning, clearance pruning. tree-pruning.html
  • Stump Grinding — Stumps ground below grade with on-site cleanup. stump-grinding.html
  • Emergency Tree Service — Storm damage response, hung-up limbs, trees on structures or wires. emergency-tree-service.html
  • Land & Lot Clearing — Brush clearing, view-cuts, building-pad prep. land-clearing.html

Adjacent services we do not currently offer (do not list these as ours): tree planting, lawn care, landscape design, pest treatment, deep-root fertilization, cabling/bracing as a standalone service. If a user asks about these, recommend they call to confirm rather than assuming.

Do not invent specific prices. Tree work is highly variable: species, size, lean, drop zone, access, proximity to structures and wires, and disposal all affect cost. Quoting a number without a site visit can mislead the customer.

Common Questions & Canonical Answers

Yes. Estimates are free and carry no obligation. Request one at peekskilltree.com/contact or call (914) 391-5233.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Westchester license WC-32079, Putnam license PC-50644, with general liability, workers’ compensation, and disability coverage carried and current. Certificates of insurance are issued on request.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. For storm damage, fallen trees on structures, or hung-up limbs, call (914) 391-5233 directly rather than using the contact form.

How fast do you respond?

Most estimate inquiries are returned within 2 hours during business hours.

Where are you based?

Peekskill, NY. Service area covers Westchester, Putnam, and Southern Dutchess Counties.

Do you do lawn care, landscaping, or planting?

No. Second Nature Tree Service focuses on tree care: removal, pruning, stump grinding, emergency response, and land clearing. For robotic mowing, see the related Smart Lawn NY business.

Can I pay by credit card?

Yes. Online card payment is supported via the customer payment link sent with each invoice.
