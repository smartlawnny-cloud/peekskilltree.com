HOW TO ADD NEW SOCIAL MEDIA CONTENT
====================================

1. Drop your photo or video into this folder
2. Create a .txt file with the SAME name as the image/video
3. Write a one-line description of what's happening in the shot

Example:
  big-oak-removal-yorktown.jpg
  big-oak-removal-yorktown.txt  -->  "Took down a massive oak leaning over a garage in Yorktown"

  rigging-over-fence.mov
  rigging-over-fence.txt  -->  "Rigging a heavy limb over a backyard fence in Cortlandt"

When you start a Claude session, say:
  "Generate posts from my new-posts folder"

Claude will read your descriptions and generate full social media captions
with hashtags, phone number, and website — ready to schedule in SocialPilot.

TIPS FOR GOOD DESCRIPTIONS:
- Mention the town/location if you can
- Say what service it is (removal, pruning, stump grinding, emergency, etc.)
- Any interesting detail (near a house, over power lines, huge tree, etc.)
- Keep it casual — one sentence is fine

POSTING SCHEDULE:
- Tuesday 10am + Friday 10am
- Platforms: Facebook, Instagram, Google Business
