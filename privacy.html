Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Second Nature Tree Service ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information you give us: name, service address, phone number, and email address.
  • Service information: details about the work you request, photos of the site, estimates, invoices, and payment records.
  • Communications: calls, text messages, and emails between you and us.
  • Website information: basic analytics such as pages viewed and referring site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to prepare estimates, schedule and perform your tree work, communicate with you about your service, send invoices and payment links, respond to your questions, and maintain our business records. We may occasionally ask you for a review.

3. Mobile Information and Text Messages

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party except the service providers that help us deliver the messages you asked for, and those providers may not use the information for any other purpose.

If you have opted in to receive text messages from us, you can stop at any time by replying STOP, or by calling (914) 391-5233. Reply HELP for assistance.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the business, and only as needed for them to perform that work. These include our messaging and email providers, our payment processor, and our accountant. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Payment Information

Card payments are processed by our payment provider. We do not store your full card number.

6. Data Retention

We keep customer and job records as long as needed to serve you and to meet tax, insurance, and legal requirements.

7. Your Choices

  • Ask us what information we have about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Opt out of text messages by replying STOP, or of email by using the unsubscribe link.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
