Crew Rates

Day rates based on 8-hour shift. Half-day (4-hour minimum) available. Rates do not include equipment unless noted.

Crew Configuration Full Day (8 hr) Half Day (4 hr) Notes
Lead Climber only $550 $300 Climbing gear & ropes included
Lead Climber + 1 Groundsman $900 $500 Ideal for pruning & light removal
Lead Climber + 2 Groundsmen $1,200 $675 Standard removal crew
